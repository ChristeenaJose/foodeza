A Little Taste of Home

FOODEZA started with something simple — missing the taste of home. We are Jobins and Christeena, originally from Kerala. What began with cooking the food we grew up with for friends and family slowly became a food truck and catering service.

Today we share the flavours of Kerala across Germany. Fresh food from our kitchen, customised menus, hot buffet, live service and delivery — for 10 to 1,000+ guests.

Because for us, food is more than a meal. It carries memories, brings people together and shares a little bit of home.
