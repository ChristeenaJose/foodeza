Our story

A Little Taste of Home

FOODEZA started with something simple — missing the taste of home.

We are Jobins and Christeena, originally from Kerala. What began with cooking the food we grew up with for friends and family slowly became a food truck and catering service.

Today, we love sharing the flavours of Kerala with people across Germany.

Because for us, food is more than a meal.

It carries memories, brings people together and shares a little bit of home.

FOODEZA — The Taste of Kerala, Served with Heart. ❤️
