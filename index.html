About Us

FOODEZA started with something very simple: missing the taste of home.

We are Jobins and Christeena, originally from Kerala, India. When we moved to Germany, we brought with us more than memories — we brought the flavours, traditions, and love of home.

We began cooking the food we grew up with for our friends and family. At first, it was simply because we loved to cook. But soon, people started asking: “When are you making this again?”, “Where can we find authentic Kerala food like this?”

Their questions, their excitement, and especially the smiles we saw around the table made us realise that our food could bring a little piece of Kerala to others too.

That is how FOODEZA began. What started in our own kitchen slowly grew into a food truck and catering service, allowing us to share the authentic flavours of Kerala and South India with people across Germany. Whether it is a family celebration, wedding, company event, festival, or a simple lunch, we still cook with the same care and passion we would have for our own family.

Because for us, food is more than a meal. It carries memories. It brings people together. It is a way of sharing love.
